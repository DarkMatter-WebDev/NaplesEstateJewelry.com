**A single cause has not been established. The available data does not support a broad disappearance from Google search.** The main site's Google clicks, U.S. clicks, mobile clicks, and gold/silver query impressions increased in comparable periods. Business Profile interactions were level. Those measurements do not disprove the owner's loss of valuable calls: they do not identify a caller's material, quality, or whether ChatGPT/Claude supplied a number that was dialed directly.

The most useful remaining hypotheses are:

1. **A change in morning availability signals or in AI referrals.** The valuable calls were commonly around 9 AM, but Google, Yelp and the jewelry satellite show an 11 AM showroom opening. The main site only added separate 9 AM–6 PM phone availability on September 8; that sentence is still absent from Google's description and Yelp's business description. An assistant or customer can infer that the business is unavailable before 11. The hours changed around the move, before the suspected decline, so delayed discovery of those hours is plausible. It is not demonstrated by a historical 9 AM ranking or AI-answer record.
2. **Additional diamond discovery may have changed the mix.** Diamond buyer was added as a seventh Google category on August 30, with a new diamond page deployed August 31. This is a documented change that could explain additional diamond discovery. No attributed-call or category-level comparison establishes that effect. There is no evidence that it displaced gold or sterling services, and it cannot explain an August 28–29 decline if that earliest date is accurate.
3. **Advertising is bringing activity that is poorly measured and may be poorly matched.** OpenAI initially targeted the United States, with Naples only in contextual hints, and optimizes clicks without conversion tracking. National targeting was corrected in the authorized follow-up below. Yelp mixes buying/selling and adjacent retail intents; none of its eight saved boosted keywords explicitly names silver, sterling or flatware. These are acquisition-quality concerns, not proof that advertising suppressed organic recommendations.
4. **Recent reputation loss may worsen an earlier slowdown.** Google now shows 18 reviews versus 23 recorded August 30. The owner says that drop occurred this past week, after calls had already slowed. Yelp shows no recommended rating and three non-recommended reviews, all about selling gold/jewelry. The owner says those Yelp reviews were always there; the date of any recommendation-status change is not known.

**Owner clarification that changes the diagnosis:** the Google opening date was changed to September 2026 **today, September 10**. It is ruled out as the original late-August cause. The current public “Add business hours” display and current opening-date field must not be projected backward into August.
